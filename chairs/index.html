No one sits

Hundreds of chairs occupy a shared structure attached to a residential building. Each has its own geometry, finish and attachment site. Local density, neighbour connections and changing construction goals drive autonomous movements. Individual components detach and exchange between compatible chairs, carrying their original finish into new assemblies.

The piece uses live 3D geometry and an adaptive assembly graph. Motion is deliberately impossible: chairs are not subject to ordinary gravity. Click a region to encourage local reconstruction.
