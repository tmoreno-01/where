Impossible material

Independent bodies move through a shared space. Some attract, others resist; contact creates temporary bridges that conduct heat and exchange volume. Hot bodies shed droplets that can be absorbed elsewhere. Four distinct surface anatomies coexist: molten lobes, braided ribbons, mineral lattices and ribbed shells.

The composition arises from these interactions rather than a fixed morph. A live chemical field modulates the material finish. Drag to orbit; tap to disturb. All geometry and behaviour are computed locally.
