# Impossible material / prototype 02
Independent real-time WebGL 2 material study. No remote computation, libraries or pretrained models.
The new surface uses a spatially warped asymmetric envelope, large cutouts, nested implicit sheets, localized molten seams, narrow conduits and selective outer skin. A continuous parameter genome steers the field, rather than crossfading preset shapes. A 48³ reaction–diffusion simulation (110,592 voxels) lives in two ping-pong texture atlases, with six-neighbour diffusion, locally varying feed/kill rates, a heat channel, and cursor perturbation. Its concentration and heat alter implicit wall thickness and material shading. The geometry is an approximate distance field, not a physical liquid simulation; reflection cards, secondary reflection occlusion and floor contact shading are approximations. RGBA16F preferred, RGBA8 fallback (lower precision and potentially different reaction dynamics). Adaptive pixel budget and raymarch steps. Desktop/mobile WebGL2 visual and performance validation remains necessary. Existing particle experiment untouched.
