# Impossible material — prototype 01
A standalone browser-native implicit-surface study, independent of the particle experiment. The shader constructs three distinct families (molten lobes, warped interlocking ribbons, perforated cast forms). A stochastic director changes parameters and blends signed-distance-like fields over time; topology can change as the zero isosurface merges and separates. Distances are conservative approximations under deformation, not exact signed distances. The studio reflection rig, floor shadow and ambient occlusion are procedural approximations. This is not a physical liquid solver or a machine-learning model.
WebGL 2, no external dependencies, no server rendering. Drag to orbit, tap to perturb, change surface finish, tempo, or generate a new form. A rendering budget adapts resolution and march steps; hidden tabs pause. Reduced motion starts paused. No changes to the existing particle artwork or production studio.
Visual verification is required on actual browsers and GPUs. In particular, watch for ray-march artifacts, transition silhouettes, floor intersections and performance on mobile.
