ABOUT THE STUDY

No one in charge

What happens when every fragment of a sculpture has its own agenda?

Each particle carries a small recurrent controller, individual preferences and a memory of its recent behaviour. It decides how much to seek, circulate or recover, adjusting its responses as conditions change. Signals pass between neighbours, allowing local decisions to affect the whole material.

Form is a consequence of these negotiations, shared physics and wider currents. Touch the field and introduce another disturbance. The work is computed live in your browser; there is no recorded sequence to return to.
